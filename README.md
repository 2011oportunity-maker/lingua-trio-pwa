# 🌍 LinguaTrio Flutter
### Native Android & iOS Vocabulary Learning App
*English 🇬🇧 • Romanian 🇷🇴 • Russian 🇷🇺*

A high-performance, 60–120 FPS native Flutter application inspired by Duolingo and Anki. Designed specifically for personal vocabulary memorization without artificial life cooldowns, ads, or paywalls.

---

## ✨ Features

- **3 Language Pair Courses**:
  - 🇷🇺 ➔ 🇷🇴 (Russian to Romanian)
  - 🇷🇺 ➔ 🇬🇧 (Russian to English)
  - 🇬🇧 ➔ 🇷🇴 (English to Romanian)
- **330+ High-Frequency Words** across 16 thematic units:
  1. Everyday & Greetings
  2. Numbers & Counting
  3. Time, Days & Calendar
  4. Food, Bakery & Cafe
  5. Fruits, Veggies & Market
  6. Travel, Transit & Directions
  7. Hotel & Accommodation
  8. Family & Relationships
  9. Home, House & Objects
  10. Common Verbs: Action & Motion
  11. Common Verbs: Thought & Speech
  12. Common Verbs: Routine & Living
  13. Key Adjectives & Descriptors
  14. Colors, Shapes & Clothes
  15. Emergency, Health & Pharmacy
  16. Connectors & Question Words
- **SM-2 Spaced Repetition (Anki-style)**:
  - 3D Matrix4 Flip Cards with smooth hardware-accelerated animations.
  - Spaced intervals (Again, Hard, Good, Easy) for permanent memorization.
- **Active Recall with Romanian Diacritics**:
  - On-screen touch toolbar for `ă`, `â`, `î`, `ș`, `ț` (and `Ă`, `Â`, `Î`, `Ș`, `Ț`).
- **Speed Match Game**:
  - Match 5 pairs under rapid recall.
- **The Learning Tree (Duolingo-style Roadmap)**:
  - Undulating path with unit banners, active pulsing nodes, and crown mastery.
- **Unlimited Mode**:
  - No heart timeouts, no ads, completely open for personal daily use.
- **100% Offline**:
  - Local database & native Android Text-to-Speech (`flutter_tts`).

## 🚀 Automated APK Compilation (GitHub Actions)

When you push this repository to GitHub:
1. GitHub Actions automatically compiles `LinguaTrio-release.apk` in ~2 minutes.
2. The APK is published directly to your repository's **Releases** tab:
   👉 `https://github.com/2011oportunity-maker/lingua-trio-pwa/releases`
3. Download it directly on your Android phone and tap **Install**!
